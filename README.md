# Blahaj Tech 1 🦈
**Blahaj Tech 1** is a technology-focused modpack with a stage-locked progression. This pack is definitely not for beginners, as it features high-complexity mechanisms and requires advanced automation in the late-game.

The name of this pack refers to the ultimate goal: the final item you must acquire to complete your journey - a **Blåhaj plushie**. To craft it, you will need to be familiar with and utilize all the technological mods to progress. Quests play an important role, acting as your guide through each chapter. With every one of them completed, you will unlock new features and tools to aid your venture toward the ultimate goal.

### ⚙️ The Path of Progress

You will advance through several key content mods in a specific order:
- **Tinker's Construct** - Upon joining a new world, you will soon notice that the game is missing every vanilla tool and most smelting recipes. This mod will aid you in overcoming this change and features very useful mechanics.
- **Immersive Engineering** - This will be your first introduction to electricity in this modpack. The main goal of this mod is acquiring steel and circuits - very important materials further in the modpack.
- **PneumaticCraft: Repressurized** - You will be able to step away from electricity for a while and focus on utilizing the air surrounding you to create more important materials necessary for the modpack's progression.
- **Thermal Series** - Stepping into the mid-game, you will be met with the Thermal Series divided into segments. With the utilization of previous mods, you will be able to create your own workplace for further progression and manage your resources with the **Applied Energistics 2** mod.
- **NuclearCraft: Overhauled** - This is where the fun begins, and you will have to start producing massive loads of electricity to be able to progress to the next chapter. With great power comes great responsibility - you will have to pay attention to what you make to avoid radiating your workplace and surroundings.
- **Quantum Minecraft Dynamics** - An end-game mod featuring very complex machines and mechanics, which may be a challenge even for experienced players. It is the final content mod which you will face; upon getting a handle on it, the Blåhaj plushie will be within reach of your hands.

### 🌠 Stellar Necessities

While the focus is on technology, you cannot reach the end through machines alone. You must also progress through **Astral Sorcery** in parallel with your technological advancements. This mod introduces unique mechanics and rewarding resources that are required to reach your goal. Harnessing the power of the constellations is just as important as mastering the atom.
